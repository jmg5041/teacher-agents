Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Teacher Agents" (the "Service"), operated by Jesse Gentile, handles information — including information accessed through Google APIs when you connect a personal AI agent to your own Google account.

1. Who we are

Teacher Agents is a pilot service that helps teachers set up personal AI agents on their own accounts and devices. It is operated solely by Jesse Gentile ("we," "us"). Contact: coming soon — this page is under development.

2. What the Service accesses

If you connect your AI agent to your Google account, the connector application ("Clem Hermes Agent Access") may request your permission to access: Gmail (read, draft, send, and organize), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, and read-only Contacts. Access is limited strictly to what you explicitly approve during Google's consent flow.

3. How data is used and stored

  • OAuth credentials and retrieved content are stored locally on your own computer, not on our servers.
  • Your agent processes your data only to carry out tasks you request (drafting email, scheduling, summarizing, organizing files).
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or any third party.
  • We do not use your data to train machine-learning models.

4. Third-party processors

Google processes your account data under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Model inference (the "AI thinking") is provided by a third-party model provider that, per its published policy, does not retain or train on submitted content. Messaging between you and your agent is handled by a messaging provider under that provider's standard terms.

5. Retention and revocation

Your agent's access persists until you revoke it. You can revoke at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates your agent's credentials. Locally stored data can be deleted by you at any time.

6. Security

Credentials are stored with file permissions restricted to your local user account. We recommend keeping your computer's disk encryption enabled (standard on modern devices).
