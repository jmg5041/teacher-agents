A personal AI agent for teachers.
Set up in a day. Yours for good.

We help teachers get their own AI agent — connected to your email, calendar, and documents — so the busywork happens after hours, in your voice.

What your agent can do

  • Parent emails, drafted in your voice — you review, it sends or saves drafts
  • Lesson plans from your own materials — your standards, your style, not generic templates
  • Calendar managed — meetings, duties, deadlines, and a morning briefing on your phone
  • Long email threads summarized — nothing slips through the cracks again

How it works

  • 1. Request pilot access — we set up a short call to understand your workflow
  • 2. We set up your agent on your accounts — you personally approve every permission it asks for
  • 3. You talk to it from your phone (Telegram), day or night

Privacy first — your data stays yours

Your agent runs against your own Google account with permissions you grant. Credentials are stored on your machine, never on ours. Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising — and you can revoke your agent's access in one click at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

About the sign-in screen ("Clem Hermes Agent Access")

When you connect your agent to Google, the approval page shows the app name "Clem Hermes Agent Access" — that's the connector app that links your agent to your account. It may request: Gmail (read, draft, send), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides, and read-only Contacts. Everything it retrieves stays on your device.
